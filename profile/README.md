## Digital Kaleidoscope Research Group

We are a group of researchers at Imperial College London who seek to understand the effects of digital technologies on (youth) health and wellbeing. We have a particular focus on digital trace data, and special expertise in video games. 

👥 Current members:
- [Nick Ballou](nickballou.com)
- Josie Brooks
- Ahuti Das Friebel
